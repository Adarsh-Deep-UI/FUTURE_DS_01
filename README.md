<h2>Business Performance Summary</h2>

Revenue Trends
- Observation: Sales showed steady growth throughout the year, peaking sharply during Q4 (holiday season).
- Driver: Increased purchasing volume in high-ticket tech products toward year-end.

Category & Product Performance
- High-Value Categories: Electronics account for ~40% of total revenue, followed by Furniture.
- Top-Selling Products: Laptops and Smartphones drive revenue due to higher unit prices, despite moderate volume sales.

Regional Performance
- Leading Markets: North America and Europe generate over 60% of total revenue combined.
- Underperforming Markets: Latin America lags in high-margin category adoption.




<h2>Strategic Recommendations</h2>

1. Inventory Planning:
   Increase inventory buffers for high-demand electronics starting in late Q3 
   to avoid stockouts during peak Q4 sales.

2. Regional Expansion:
   Launch targeted promotional campaigns in lower-performing regions 
   (e.g., Latin America) focused on mid-tier products to boost market penetration.

3. Cross-Selling Opportunities:
   Bundle high-margin items (e.g., Laptops) with lower-cost accessories 
   (e.g., Office Supplies) to increase the overall Average Order Value (AOV).
